# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
Swift Loop a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this a friendly, kind, and welcoming space
for designers, developers, and anyone curious enough to open a PR.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with people, especially newcomers.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them.
- Focusing on what's best for the community, not just ourselves.

Examples of behavior that isn't welcome:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behavior that breaks these standards, please reach out
to Mia Holte at **mia@swiftner.com**. All reports will be handled with
discretion and respect for the privacy of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issue
tracker, pull requests, and any official channels — and also when an individual
is representing the project in public.
